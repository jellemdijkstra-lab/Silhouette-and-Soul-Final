Maternity Photography Timing: When's the Best Week to Book?

One of the very first questions I get from expecting moms is some version of: "When should I actually book this?" It's a great question, and the honest answer is — earlier than you think.

The sweet spot: 28 to 34 weeks.

This window tends to be the most flattering and the most comfortable for the vast majority of clients. Your belly is beautifully round and well-defined by this point, but you're not yet in the final, most physically exhausting stretch of the third trimester. You'll have the energy to enjoy the experience, hold a pose comfortably, and feel present for the whole session.

Why not wait until closer to your due date?

By 36+ weeks, many moms are dealing with swelling, fatigue, and general discomfort that can make a session feel like a chore rather than a celebration. There's also the very real risk of early labour pushing your due date up — and a maternity session can't happen after baby arrives. Booking a little earlier gives you breathing room.

Why not book too early?

If you book before 26 weeks or so, your bump often hasn't fully "popped" yet, which can mean less definition in the photos than you might be hoping for. There are exceptions — every pregnancy is different — but generally, a little patience pays off here.

When should you actually call the studio?

Here's the part people don't always think about: you should reach out for your consultation well before you want the shoot itself. Studio calendars fill up, especially during popular due-date seasons, and I want plenty of time to talk through your vision, your wardrobe, and your ideal timeline — no rushing.

A good rule of thumb: reach out as soon as you know you want maternity photos, even if that's in your second trimester. We'll map out the right week together based on your due date, your comfort, and the look you're going for.
