Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before you book. If your question isn't here, call (250) 215-3530 or schedule a complimentary consultation. It's just a conversation.

Getting started

What happens at the complimentary consultation?

add

It's a relaxed conversation, by phone or in my West Kelowna studio. We talk through your vision, wardrobe and styling, and any worries you have. There's no commitment and no pressure. You'll leave knowing exactly what to expect on the day.

Can I bring someone with me?

add

Yes, and I ask every client to. Bring a partner, a friend or a sister, whoever makes you feel safest. They're with you from the start. You're in control at every moment: if you want to pause, adjust or skip something, just say so. No explanation needed.

How much does a session cost?

add

The creative fee is $489. It includes your consultation, the fully guided session, and 5 fully retouched images with a digital print release. Additional images are $50 each, with no minimum purchase. Most clients invest between $600 and $1,000 in total. See the pricing page for the full breakdown.

What is included in the session fee?

add

A pre-session style and concept consultation, a fully guided session with professional lighting and posing direction, expert retouching, and 5 retouched digital images.

Boudoir

I'm nervous. Is boudoir for me?

add

Almost everyone who walks in is nervous, and that's completely expected. Every pose, angle and moment is guided, so you never have to figure anything out. You don't need experience, and you don't need to feel confident beforehand. Confidence is usually what you leave with. Read more on the boudoir page.

What should I wear for my boudoir session?

add

Choose pieces that make you feel comfortable: lingerie, a robe, a bodysuit or simple draped fabric. I also have a client wardrobe with lingerie, robes, bodysuits and wraps in a range of sizes, and we go through the options together at your consultation. Neutral tones and soft textures photograph beautifully in my light and shadow style.

Are my photos kept private?

add

Yes. Your images are never shared unless you give written permission, and that permission is given separately for each image.

Maternity

When should I book my maternity session?

add

The ideal window is 28 to 34 weeks, when your belly is beautifully defined and you can still move and pose comfortably. Sessions at 35 or 36 weeks are very doable too. I recommend booking by week 25 for the most flexibility. If you're closer to your due date, reach out anyway, because I may have last-minute availability. More on the maternity page.

Can my partner or children join?

add

Yes. We spend the first part of the session on you alone, then bring your partner or little ones in for the connection shots. Just tell me when you book.

Portraits and headshots

Do you offer professional headshots?

add

Yes. Most clients book a headshot and a portrait in the same session. We start with clean, polished frames for LinkedIn or your website, then shift into a more expressive, editorial direction. See the portraits page.

What's the difference between a headshot and a portrait?

add

A headshot is a professional tool: clean, polished and consistent. A portrait is more personal. It's about character and presence rather than credentials. You leave with images that work professionally and images that feel true.

Can I use my headshot on LinkedIn?

add

Yes. Your headshots are yours to use on your LinkedIn profile.

Sessions and delivery

How long does a session take?

add

Maternity sessions run 60 to 90 minutes, and portrait sessions 45 to 75 minutes. Boudoir sessions vary slightly depending on how many looks you'd like. That's enough time for outfit changes, different lighting and guided posing without feeling rushed.

How do I receive my final images?

add

After your session, you'll receive access to a private online gallery where you can view and select your images. Your fully edited images are typically ready in about two weeks. They're delivered digitally in high resolution, ready to print or share. Fine art prints and wall art are available to order.

Do you shoot outdoors or on location?

add

Silhouette & Soul sessions take place in my private studio, where controlled, directional light gives every image the same fine art quality in any season. For outdoor work, I run a separate business, Jelle Dijkstra Photography. It covers weddings and elopements, commercial and brand photography, and family and portrait sessions, shot in natural light across Kelowna, West Kelowna and the Okanagan. If you want golden hour, a vineyard or a mountain backdrop instead of the studio, that's the place to start.

Where is the studio, and who do you serve?

add

My private studio is at 3269 McLeod Road in West Kelowna, by appointment only. I regularly work with clients from Kelowna, Peachland, Lake Country and across the Okanagan, and many travel specifically for the private, guided experience.
