5 Reasons to Book a Maternity Session (Even If You Feel Self-Conscious)

"I'll book a session once I feel more like myself." It's one of the most common things I hear from expecting moms — and it's exactly why I'm writing this.

Pregnancy doesn't wait for you to feel confident, and neither should your photos. Here are five reasons to book your maternity session now, even if "self-conscious" is the word running through your head.

1. You don't need to feel confident to look confident.

This is the part most people don't expect: confidence isn't a prerequisite for these photos — it's the result of them. My job is to guide every pose, every angle, every moment of the shoot. You don't need experience, a plan, or even a clear idea of what you want. You just need to show up.

2. This version of you only exists for a few months.

However you feel about your body right now, this exact season of your life — this belly, this glow, this anticipation — is temporary in the most beautiful way. Years from now, you won't remember feeling "not ready." You'll just be grateful you have the photos.

3. Soft, intentional lighting changes everything.

A huge part of feeling self-conscious in photos comes from bad lighting and stiff, unnatural posing — the kind you might remember from a quick phone photo. In studio, with fine art lighting designed specifically to flatter and soften, you see yourself in a completely different way. Most clients tell me the reveal is the moment it finally clicks.

4. The reveal is designed to feel safe.

There's no scrolling through hundreds of unedited, unflattering shots. You'll sit down with me in person, view your fully retouched gallery, and choose the images that speak to you. It's intimate, it's guided, and it's honestly one of my favourite parts of this whole experience.

5. Your partner and future family will treasure these.

These images aren't just for you in this moment — they become part of your family's story. A quiet, powerful reminder of the season you carried your baby, to look back on for decades.

If "self-conscious" is the only thing in your way, let's talk it through. A complimentary consultation is a no-pressure first step — just a conversation about your vision, your worries, and what this experience could look like for you.
