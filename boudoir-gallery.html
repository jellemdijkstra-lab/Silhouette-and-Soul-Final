THE SESSION

What to expect: before, during, and after

What if I'm nervous?

Almost every client walks in nervous. That's not a problem. It's expected. The nervousness usually fades within the first ten minutes once you realise that every single thing is guided: where to put your hands, how to angle your body, when to hold still. You don't need experience. You don't need to feel confident going in. Confidence is usually what you leave with.

Do I need experience in front of a camera?

No. In fact, clients who have never been photographed this way often produce the most honest, , because they're not performing a version of what they think a boudoir photo should look like. My job is to find the pose and the light that works for your body, not to make you fit a template. Every session is built from scratch around you.

What happens at the reveal?

You don't get a link to a folder of unedited photos. You sit down with me in person or over Zoom, view your fully retouched gallery on a large screen, and choose the images that speak to you. It's one of my : the moment most clients say "I didn't know I could look like that." The reveal is private, unhurried, and completely on your terms.

Bring someone with you

You're welcome to bring a partner, a friend or a sister. Someone who makes you laugh, who makes you feel safe. They're with you from the start, and their being there makes a real difference. You're in control at every moment: if you want to pause, adjust or step back, just say so. No explanation needed.

"Every woman I photograph already has everything. My only job is to help her see what I see."
